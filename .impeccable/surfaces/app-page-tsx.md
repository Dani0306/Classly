# Landing + app shell

Scope: `/` (Persuade) and the `/app` shell and pages (Operate). Audience: students managing several classes. Action: Start For Free (Google sign-in). Constraints: keep Sora, Classly name/logo, existing copy.

## Direction contract

THESIS: Every piece of study material is a sticky note on a dark study wall; AI turns a scribbled note into a clean one. Refuses the centered-headline-over-screenshot SaaS page and the white card grid.

OWN-WORLD: Charcoal felt board (#22252b, fine dot texture) as ground. Fluorescent paper notes: canary #ffe94a, pink #ff6fb5, sky #49c6ff, lime #a8f05a, plain paper #f4f4f1, ink #17181c on every note. Notes are square-cornered paper with a translucent tape strip and an offset soft shadow. Sora 800 at tight tracking for headlines. Buttons are small paper notes (lime primary, paper secondary).

STORY: The visitor sees the headline literally written on notes, watches a messy Biology note become an AI summary, understands per-class organization and AI tools, sees two plans, starts free.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Header on the board (logo, three links, Sign In note). "Turn messy notes" on a canary note top-left at ~92px, "into mastery" on a pink note overlapping below-right, subcopy on a paper note, CTAs as lime and paper notes beside it. Right third: a sky "your note" and a paper "AI summary" note joined by an arrow.

FORM: Sticky Wall, my top-ranked grounded candidate (1 of 7), chosen by the user over the assigned Planner. seed:c030f72cb4545600. Signature interaction: notes rest slightly tilted and straighten and lift on hover/focus; in the app tilt is subtle so grids stay scannable.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
